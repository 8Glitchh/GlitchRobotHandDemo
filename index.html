Robotic Hand
Hardware & embeddedhidden layer · actuation

  

    

      
A 3D-printed robotic hand that copies human finger movement. I engineered the electronics and the working mechanics.

      
It takes two kinds of input: a wearable glove with a flex sensor on every finger, or a laptop camera that tracks your fingers. An Arduino Uno turns each reading into an angle for one of five SG90 servos, and the servos pull tensioned fishing lines that run through the fingers like tendons.
